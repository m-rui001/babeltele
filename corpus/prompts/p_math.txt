Problem. Let n be a positive integer. Determine, with proof, the largest possible number of pairs (a, b) of elements of a set S of n positive integers with a < b such that a + b divides a*b. Give the exact extremal value as a function of n and characterize the sets attaining it.
Solve it the way a research mathematician would: search for structure, try small n by hand, form a conjecture, then prove it. Explicitly record the dead ends and the moment the key invariant appears. State any lemma you use and prove it. Reason out loud, showing every arithmetic check you make.
