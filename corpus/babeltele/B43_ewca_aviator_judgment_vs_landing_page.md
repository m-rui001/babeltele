Judgment Approved by the court for handing down.

The issue on this appeal is what law applies to a plea of res judicata, or more specifically issue estoppel, based on a foreign judgment in a case concerning claims for copyright infringement allegedly committed in numerous territories. The Claimants and the Fifth Party (collectively “Aviator”) contend that English law applies as the lex fori (the law of the forum). The Defendant (“Spribe”) contends that the law applicable to each claim for copyright infringement, the lex loci protectionis (the law of the country in respect of which protection is claimed), applies by virtue of European Parliament and Regulation 864/2007/EC of 11 July 2007 on the law applicable to non-contractual obligations (“the Rome II Regulation”). Subject to minor amendments made by regulation 11 of the Law Applicable to Contractual Obligations and Non-Contractual Obligations (Amendment etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019 (SI 2019/234), the Rome II Regulation is assimilated law.

The First Claimant and the Fifth Party are companies incorporated in Georgia, and the Sixth Party is a citizen of Georgia. The Second and Third Claimants are companies incorporated in Malta.

A crash game is an online gambling game where players place bets on the outcome of a rapidly increasing multiplier. The goal is to cash out before the multiplier “crashes”

In January 2019 a company associated with Aviator launched a crash game in Georgia under the logo reproduced below (“the Aviator Image”). 6.

This may be contrasted with the abuse of process doctrine which originated in Henderson v Henderson (1843) 3 Hare 100, which is “a concept which informs the exercise of the court’s procedural powers”. 23.

For the reasons given above I would allow the appeal. Lord Justice Miles: 61. I agree. Lady Justice King: 62. I also agree.
