PIE and CoDel are two recently proposed Active Queue Management (AQM) algorithms, designed to tackle bufferbloat by lowering the queuing delay without degrading the bottleneck utilization.

CoDel has been shown to have auto-tuning issues and its performance is sensitive to the trafﬁc load [7]. Also, its default 5 ms of maximum allowed queuing delay can be damaging for low-speed bottlenecks [8] and its interval value is based on the assumption that the Round Trip Time (RTT) is 100 ms [9].
