If MFA is enabled passwords shorter than 8 characters are considered to be weak (NIST SP800-63B). Note that certain implementations of hashing algorithms may cause long password denial of service. Longer passphrases are effective because they raise the number of guesses an attacker's dictionary or wordlist has to cover, not because of a precise entropy value: NIST notes that "estimating entropy for user-chosen passwords is challenging" and recommends length and blocklist checks (see below) over composition or entropy math (NIST SP800-63B, Strength of Passwords). Entropy estimates rely on assumptions about the search space and should be treated as illustrative rather than as absolute measures of password strength. Attackers commonly use password guessing techniques that leverage common-password dictionaries and password lists, and may use previously compromised credentials in credential stuffing attacks.
